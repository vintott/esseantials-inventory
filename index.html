Important Rules & Penalties

Eligibility Requirements

  • Exclusively accessible to currently enrolled students under the School of Engineering and Architecture (SEA).
  • Possession and drop collection of an authentic HAU Student ID is strictly mandatory.

Borrowing Terms & Limits

  • Each individual student profile is limited to a maximum threshold of 5 separate item borrows per month.
  • Same-day return conditions apply unconditionally unless custom administrative staff overrides are logged.

Late Returns & Care Guidelines

  • Any check-ins post 5:00 PM or keeping devices overnight will register an automatic system infraction penalty.
  • Accumulating a total of 3 separate late return infractions will trigger a temporary freeze on your borrowing privileges.
  • Please return drafting items completely clean. Ensure all surfaces are wiped free of graphite trace lines or ink markings.

Loss & Damage Liability

  • Borrowers bear absolute liability for equipment damage or unit replacement costs calculated by the council team.
  • Unresolved profile citations drop into automatic academic escalation pipelines directly reaching your department supervisors.

Reporting Concerns

  • Immediate item errors or missing hardware components must be brought up right away to our counter staff, the Vice Governor, or addressed to the official Facebook page platform interface.
