Borrowing Steps & Guidelines

  • 1

    Message the Facebook Page

    Send a direct message to the official esSEAntials Facebook page stating the specific model item you need.

    Average queue validation response time: 10–15 minutes
  • 2

    Proceed to the CSC-SEA Office

    Once your reservation is confirmed via chat, head to the office located at the APS 3rd Floor, near the bridge.

    Operational hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • 3

    Verification & ID Collateral

    Review the standard agreements, sign the physical Borrowing Logbook, and leave your valid HAU Student ID with the officer.

  • 4

    Receive Equipment

    Inspect the device details before leaving. Remember, all gear is strictly provisioned for on-campus configurations only.

  • 5

    Timely Return Checkpoint

    Return your assigned equipment back to the desk on the same day prior to the closing cutoff time.

    Strict Return Deadline: 5:00 PM daily
  • 6

    Feedback Record

    Complete the brief 1-minute quality feedback form at the counter to claim back your HAU Student ID card.

Important Rules & Penalties

Eligibility Constraints

  • Exclusively accessible to currently enrolled students under the School of Engineering and Architecture (SEA).
  • Possession and deposit of an authentic HAU Student ID is mandatory.

Borrowing Terms & Limits

  • Each individual student is capped at a maximum of 5 lending transactions per month.
  • Same-day return guidelines apply unconditionally unless explicitly authorized by technical administrative staff.

Late Returns & Device Care

  • Any device return logs registered past 5:00 PM or kept overnight will be automatically penalized as late.
  • Accumulating 3 late marks will result in a temporary suspension of all borrowing network clearance profiles.
  • Instruments must be turned in clean. Please ensure drafting surfaces are thoroughly cleared of any ink or graphite residues.

Loss & Damage Liability

  • Borrowers bear absolute liability for damage issues. Consequences require either an exact device model replacement or matching component compensation payments.
  • Unresolved violations escalate directly to council management and corresponding academic department supervisors.

Reporting Protocols

  • Immediate damage system failures must be flagged right away to counter attendants, the Vice Governor, or submitted directly via the official esSEAntials Facebook channel page.
